01 — Concept

The Pull

The mark abstracts the instinct to call something closer — the doubled, inward-scooping gesture of invitation. Not a hand, not an animal. Pure motion.

Four outer nodes represent people at the edge of discovery. Curved trajectories sweep toward a central focal point — the shared experience that creates gravity, curiosity, and community.

Negative space between the arcs forms a subtle funnel: attraction made geometry.
