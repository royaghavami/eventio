02 — Brand Story

The Pull

Pish Pish connects people with creative events, pop-up markets, workshops, performances, and brand collaborations. The brand is built on a single instinct: the magnetic pull of something worth showing up for.

Four curved purple segments orbit a black center — shared energy, the experience everyone converges on. Tapered pull lines between asymmetric outer anchors communicate attraction and tension without literal illustration.

The open-counter wordmark echoes the mark's openness: invitation, curiosity, room for people to gather.

12 — Usage Guidelines

Do

  • Use approved SVG assets at all sizes
  • Maintain minimum clearspace around the mark
  • Use white symbol on dark and purple backgrounds
  • Keep the purple focal point intact
  • Use horizontal lockup for web headers

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, or skew the logo
  • Change symbol colors outside the palette
  • Add drop shadows, gradients, or outlines
  • Place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Combine with ticket, pin, or calendar icons
